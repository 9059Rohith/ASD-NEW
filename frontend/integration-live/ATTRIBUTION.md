# Short-E browser test recording

`quiet-short-e-once.wav` is derived from `backend/models/vowel-classifier/heldout/e.wav` by adding two seconds of leading silence and eight seconds of trailing silence. The voiced recording is unchanged.

Source: Revathi Arunachalam, Vijayakrishnan VK, Nandhakumar N and Akilan A (2025), *Tamil vowels-speech database*, version 1, Mendeley Data, https://doi.org/10.17632/2dnxmvm22k.1.
