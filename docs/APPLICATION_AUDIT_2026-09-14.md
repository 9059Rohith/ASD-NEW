# Tamil application audit — 2026-09-14

This is a status record for the running local application, not a claim that every item in the master execution prompt is complete. The source inventory covered 393 files in the active frontend, backend, Android, and launcher directories. Browser smoke visited 48 declared route patterns and the 404 fallback with mocked route data; focused tests exercised actual Tamil APIs and selected interactive routes.

## Implemented and checked

- The Tamil API catalog now contains 12 vowels, 18 consonants, 216 generated consonant–vowel combinations, 9 beginner words, and 8 sentences. IDs and Unicode sequences are validated by backend tests. The legacy 16-lesson therapy catalog and its dashboard/progress links now agree on lesson 16, PAZHAM.
- Every vowel has a local human-recorded example. All 17 word and sentence examples have local generated Tamil audio. The 29 WAVs have a provenance manifest and pass byte/format validation; all 29 URLs returned WAV data through the running web server.
- Study pages, character/text recognition, a local-audio listening challenge, honest speech/text comparison, server-graded answers, idempotent saves, progress, and three-correct-answer recognition mastery are connected. Acoustic scores remain separate from recognition answers. Browser speech-to-text shows its actual transcript and is explicitly optional.
- Word and sentence meaning matching, combined-letter construction from consonant and vowel, sentence word ordering, and typed Tamil writing are server-graded and saved with the same idempotency contract. Writing compares NFC-normalized script without discarding word boundaries. A review queue derived from persisted answers schedules successful practice after 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days and makes a missed item due immediately.
- Speech transcript comparison now preserves sentence word boundaries and rejects extra non-Tamil text. A further false-success case showed that the old two-edit sentence tolerance accepted the catalog word `அப்பா` in place of `அம்மா` in a longer sentence. The current source requires exact normalized Tamil text for correctness while retaining similarity as partial feedback; backend and browser regression tests cover both one-character variations and the meaningful word swap. New saved speech-text results are versioned `v2`.
- The Android client uses Tamil script with a `ta-IN` voice when available, stops recording in the background, derives progress/rewards from backend evidence, and passed its offline debug build and six unit tests.
- Calendar, Appointments, and Rewards now use backend data and mutation APIs instead of fabricated schedules, therapists, bookings, or balances. The calendar includes completed and missed sessions; the appointments API excludes concluded records from the default upcoming view. Route recovery and legacy learning navigation were repaired. The web dependency audit reports zero vulnerabilities after scoped overrides and tool updates.

## Remaining release work

- Consonant and combined-letter lessons cannot claim validated isolated-letter pronunciation scores. They provide text study, recognition and combined-letter construction practice, and browser Tamil TTS only when such a voice exists; unlike vowels/words/sentences, they have no bundled local audio. Audio examples for all 234 letters are not yet complete.
- [Tamil Virtual Academy says its alphabet table plays all 247 letters](https://www.tamilvu.org/ta/courses-beginner-help-finalhelpl-3106), but its [FAQ grants free viewing/downloading for learning while separately restricting commercial ebook redistribution](https://www.tamilvu.org/en/coresite-html-cwfaqqueen-342478). That does not establish rights to repackage the letter recordings in this application, so none were copied into its assets.
- [MLCommons Multilingual Spoken Words](https://mlcommons.org/datasets/multilingual-spoken-words/) offers a Tamil split under CC BY 4.0. The 12,477,230-byte `audio/ta.tar.gz` download (SHA-256 `8ee5f98975a35493ea74d384187ecdc123ea1a83580635a2406369a1b87131a5`) contains 190 labeled words and 1,884 one-second Opus clips. Candidate beginner labels include `நல்ல`, `பெரிய`, `சின்ன`, and `பேச`. A local phoneme-model spot check of one clip per label returned extra or differing sounds, consistent with possible neighboring speech in automatically segmented snippets; the model is not an independent Tamil listening judge. These clips were **not** added as lesson reference audio. A Tamil-speaking reviewer should approve exact clip boundaries and labels before any are bundled. The no-speaker-identification download condition was observed.
- The 9-word and 8-sentence beginner set is a small foundation, not an extensive curriculum. Sentence ordering, meaning matching, and typed writing are implemented, but freehand handwriting recognition and a broader curriculum remain outstanding. The review queue is interval-based and has not been validated as a measure of long-term retention.
- The 17 generated audio examples carry a noncommercial license. Commercial distribution needs replacement audio or separate rights clearance.
- Browser route smoke uses mocked APIs; it does not prove every control against live data. On-device Android microphone/TTS validation and independent human speech-scoring validation remain outstanding.
- The vowel model has a speaker-disjoint runtime evaluation of 200 human test segments: 80.5% of segments received a score, and 83.85% of scored segments had the correct combined identity and length. Short O and U were weakest (8/15 and 8/13 correct among scored segments respectively). These metrics are evidence of a real, limited classifier, not proof of clinical or dialect-general accuracy. A locally cached multilingual Whisper tiny model matched only 2 of 17 shipped synthetic Tamil word/sentence clips in a CPU smoke check, so it was not connected as an STT fallback. The reproducible local-model diagnostic is `backend/scripts/benchmark_tamil_asr.py`; its output is in `.runlogs/tamil-whisper-tiny-benchmark.json` and is not an independent human benchmark. The configured IndicConformer checkpoint is present, but its NeMo runtime is absent here; live `/health/speech` exposes that limitation explicitly. A larger MIT-licensed [Whisper large-v3-turbo conversion](https://huggingface.co/dropbox-dash/faster-whisper-large-v3-turbo) was considered, but a 1 MB range took about eight seconds; its 1.62 GB weight download was stopped before model evaluation.
- A read-only/idempotent migration for older permissive speech-text results is in `backend/scripts/regrade_tamil_text_attempts.py`, with tests that correct a false sentence success and leave exact matches intact. A successful dry run against the local development database examined zero v1 speech-text attempts, so no migration writes were needed. The configured Atlas connection in `backend/.env` had intermittent TLS failures, including a failed live answer save; the local launcher overrides it with the already-running local MongoDB. The updated main API was restarted with that local configuration, reported ready, and passed the complete live Tamil browser journey.
- The therapy route now loads a roughly 14 kB entry chunk and lazy-loads later slides. The 3D candle step still produces an approximately 804 kB minified chunk and a build warning; that step needs further optimization for slow networks.
- The local Python runtime inherits system packages and reports many unrelated global advisories. Auditing only `requirements-core.txt` resolves to one distinct [moderate scikit-learn advisory](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-jw8x-6495-233v) (listed twice by the advisory feed) in the pinned 1.4 series. Its affected `TfidfVectorizer` feature is not used by this app, but the pin should be revisited when the serialized acoustic models are retrained or validated on a newer scikit-learn version. Production release should use an isolated build.
